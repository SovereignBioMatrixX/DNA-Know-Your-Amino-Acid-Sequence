# DNA Know Your Amino Acid Sequence
**Discovering the Quiet Grammar of Life — From Heredity to Molecular Coding**

## 📖 About the Book
Before a child is born, before a leaf unfurls, before a bacterium divides, the instructions are already there. Folded into a molecule so small that it cannot be seen with ordinary eyes, and yet so powerful that it shapes every inheritance, every protein, every cell, every living body. That molecule is DNA. 

This work serves as an intellectual journey from the familiar concepts of family resemblance and inherited traits to the deep, rigorous mechanics of molecular biology. It untangles standard confusions for learners and scientists alike, answering vital foundational questions: what the name means, where the molecule was discovered, why it became so important, how it operates as hereditary material, and the precise definitions separating DNA, genes, and genomes.
